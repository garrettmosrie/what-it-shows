The two causes

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. Only dates whose day is 12 or less can flip, which is why it looks random from the outside. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the queued fix is for it. Ticket #1.

The note under the table names the reports the desk read as a one-day move: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” (R-41202). A day/month swap cannot move a date by one day. They are counted here, where the desk files them, and #1 lists them. When the parser fix ships, check them first: if they still happen, they are a bug of their own, most likely a time zone (one meeting showed Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, R-41344).

hint: booking-v3’s form tells day-first tenants to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At a day-first tenant, people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap, but the code is different: the parser fix does not touch it. Only reports that came in through the form at a booking-v3 tenant have it. Ticket #2.

What changes on the desk

  • #4471 keeps parser. New hint reports go to #2.
  • Place a report by tenant, channel and form, not by intake’s tag.
