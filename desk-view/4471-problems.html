The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. Only dates whose day is 12 or less can flip, which is why it looks random from the outside. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells day-first tenants to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At a day-first tenant, people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap, but the code is different: the parser fix does not touch it. Only reports that came in through the form at a booking-v3 tenant have it.

one-day: dates that move by one day

Some reports are not a swap at all. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” (R-41202). A day/month swap cannot move a date by one day, so this is a different bug. It looks like a time-zone conversion: one meeting showed Tuesday to a client in Lisbon and Monday to a client in Toronto (R-41344). The desk spotted these (desk-notes.json), but the cause table files them under parser by tenant. Here they are counted apart.
